The Gunslinger

72 HPweathered brass #d9a05bthe Cylindercalm / dangerous
Playing: Idle / gait

Stance

Contrapposto, angled three-quarters rather than squared to the viewer — a duelist's stance, not a soldier's. Weight sits on the back leg; the front leg is soft, ready to shift. The gun hand is bent tight and held near the hip, hovering just above the holster; the off hand hangs loose.

Movement

Idle is mostly still — a slow, low-amplitude sway plus a long coat-tail flutter and a small fast tremor in the gun-hand forearm, the one part of this character that hasn't fully decided to stay calm. Attack is fast and snappy: the gun hand whips up into aim and kicks back on the shot in under half a second, no wind-up — this class doesn't telegraph. Self buff is a confident cylinder spin: the gun hand rotates through a full flourish while the body leans back a beat, a small showman's gesture between exchanges.

Component map — the grand vision

A structural prototype, not a game asset — same spirit as concept-art/. Real combat rigs are generated by the external sts2-reskin-pipeline workbench into spine/gunslinger/; this is a design reference for that pipeline's edits.json (stance/posture/proportions) and its art_in/ (part art), not a replacement for either. See design/gunslinger.md for the mechanical identity this stance is built from. Every part below is a procedurally drawn placeholder icon in the character palette — no reference art exists yet for this character, unlike the Paladin's head.
