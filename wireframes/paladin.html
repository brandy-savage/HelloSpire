The Paladin

75 HPgold #e8c46aSeals / Judgmentco-op class
Playing: Idle / gait

Stance

Square to the viewer, feet planted wide, knees almost locked — a stance built to not be moved. Spine dead straight, chest up. The shield arm (viewer's left) is drawn in tight and bent hard, guard-height; the weapon arm hangs nearly straight, relaxed but ready. Nothing about the silhouette suggests retreat — this is the class the design doc calls "durable but slow to kill."

Movement

Idle is a slow, heavy breathing sway (~7s cycle) through the spine, head, and both shoulders — armor moving as one mass — layered with a slower shield-arm "readiness" flex (~20s cycle). Attack is a two-handed mace swing: a wind-up that draws the weapon arm back and up, a hard strike with torso torque, then settle. Self buff is Judgment: the shield raises and presents, the head tilts up, and the chest sigil pulses — the Seals firing. Both return to idle automatically; the legs and hips keep their own idle sway underneath either one, since only the bones each move actually poses stop breathing on their own.

Component map — the grand vision
